# ADR-010 — Preserve the reconstructed head with a fitted semantic face

**Status:** Accepted for M7 architecture; M8 must validate automatic fitting and visual quality

## Context

M6 has produced an accepted complete-head Hunyuan reconstruction. The real GLB inspected for M7 has one textured 457,430-triangle primitive, no morph targets, no skin, no animation, and no semantic part labels. After exact-position welding, it is one closed surface. This is **one confirmed sample**, not a topology contract for future Hunyuan results: other people can produce different vertex counts, triangle layouts, UV seams, geometry, materials and proportions. M7 must select an automatic path to the required blinks, gaze, brows, jaw, lips, mouth interior and future visemes while preserving each avatar's 360° skull, hair, ears and identity. The product has no application backend and requires a WebGL2/Safari runtime.

## Decision

Use a **hybrid identity shell plus fitted deformable face**. Run preparation independently for **each** M6 reconstruction. Keep that avatar's original immutable. Fit a standardized semantic facial patch to its own identity surface, construct independent eyes and mouth interior, integrate the patch into its own head with a validated seamless transition, and export a separate prepared GLB plus semantic manifest. The ICT template supplies the stable semantic topology; **no Hunyuan vertex index, face boundary, UV island or material assignment is assumed to match another avatar**. The per-avatar head/hair/ears/neck geometry supplies the 360° identity shell and shares one `HeadRoot` with the deformable parts. `AvatarRigAdapter` consumes only `AvatarControlState` and the manifest; it never receives MediaPipe landmarks or arbitrary Hunyuan indices.

Use the **MIT-licensed ICT FaceKit Light topology and named expression set as the initial template source**, not its differently licensed Full model. Its published full facial region, mouth socket, eyes, teeth, tongue and 53 ARKit-like expressions directly cover the semantic control families; M8 must adapt the neutral shape and deltas to stylized identity, not use the stock realistic face unchanged. The sampled full-face outer boundary moves less than the narrow-face boundary under jaw and lip morphs, so use the full facial region as the first fitting patch and propagate remaining boundary motion into a short transition band. Keep hair, ears and rear skull outside that band. Retain Google GNM Head as a technical comparison/fallback template if ICT geometry cannot meet the quality gate; adopting it would require an explicit update to this decision.

The preparation is intended as deterministic local browser processing, off the render loop, potentially using a Web Worker and portable WASM computation. No custom backend, per-avatar Blender session, live model inference, or WebGPU is required by the chosen architecture. M8 will implement and measure the builder; M7 adds only the research probe and decision documentation.

## Alternatives and evidence

- **Specialized animatable-head model/provider:** [GNM Head](https://github.com/google/GNM/blob/main/gnm/shape/README.md) has rich parameterized anatomy; [Didimo](https://developer.didimo.co/docs/mesh) and [Avatar SDK](https://docs.avatarsdk.com/standalone-app/3.1.2/parameters_description.html) provide animatable head assets. Their documented paths generate/fit their own heads. They do not demonstrate preservation of this approved stylized Hunyuan mesh and hair. Replacing the head fails the identity-shell requirement.
- **Standard full-head template fitting:** [ICT FaceKit Light](https://github.com/USC-ICT/ICT-FaceKit) provides known topology and semantic shapes. Replacing the _whole_ head would discard the distinctive Hunyuan rear skull/hair/accessories; regional fitting retains them. [Nonrigid ICP](https://iacl.ece.jhu.edu/proceedings/cvpr2007/papers/0197.pdf) is a published route to fitting a common template, with correspondences and stiffness that still need robust implementation.
- **Transfer morphs onto the entire generated mesh:** [Deformation transfer](https://people.csail.mit.edu/sumner/research/deftransfer/) is technically possible with correspondences, but the actual UV-split, high-density, closed Hunyuan surface lacks eye and oral anatomy. A naive 53-target position set is ~183 MB. It does not remove the need for semantic detection, new interior geometry and strong quality gates. Transfer remains useful _within_ the fitted patch.
- **Provider auto-rig:** [fal Meshy rigging](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/meshy/rigging/api) requires a humanoid with limbs and publishes body rig/animation outputs, not head-only facial morphs. [OmniFaceRig](https://omnifacerig.github.io/) provides strong research evidence for an automatic template-registration / inner-mouth / expression-transfer architecture across diverse static characters, but its public page currently offers no usable inference code or service. Its results cannot be assumed for this asset.
- **FLAME template:** [FLAME](https://github.com/TimoBolkart/FLAME-Universe) supports a facial model and fitting, but its model requires a separate license agreement; it is not the default redistributable template for this product.

The isolated M7 GLB and ICT template probes and candidate scoring, including the exact inspected asset fingerprint, are in [M7 research record](../research/m7-avatar-preparation.md). The template probe compared neutral and seven real expression OBJs: identical topology, four facial boundary loops, and a `jawOpen` outer-boundary displacement of 1.402 template units for the narrow patch versus 0.181 for the full face. These observations justify both the full-face patch and a moving transition band; they do **not** prove a seamless fit to Hunyuan. The real-asset probe confirms that direct MediaPipe-to-vertex binding would be unjustified and that a small morphable region is much more plausible than morphing all 287,705 indexed vertices. The [glTF specification](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html) and [Three.js GLTFLoader](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/GLTFLoader.html) support the intended portable asset format; actual Safari shape count and performance need M8/M11 measurement.

## Tradeoffs

This path keeps the hardest problem visible: automatically locating the stylized face, fitting its exact eye/lip proportions, cutting the rigid source surface, reproducing its texture, and preventing a seam through extreme expressions. The local fitting computation may be too slow on some devices. The template's default human anatomy may need controlled stylization, and not all 53 expressions will be useful or safe in the initial runtime asset. These are M8 acceptance risks, not reasons to accept a face-only or 2.5D substitute. A failed preparation must be rejected with reasons rather than shown as a valid avatar.

## Consequences

M8 implements the browser-side preparation pipeline, semantic manifest, optimized derived GLB, geometric/visual rejection gates and synthetic-pose evaluation across multiple identities with differing topology, hair and facial proportions. It may not rewrite any original M6 bytes. A successful fit of the one inspected GLB is insufficient to claim generality; each new avatar needs an independent fit and a reject/retry outcome when confidence or quality is low. The future tracking interpreter (M9) maps observations into `AvatarControlState` only. The renderer continues to consume prepared rig adapters, independent of ICT, Hunyuan, fal, MediaPipe and the fitting implementation. The fundamental architecture is chosen here; any substitution of the facial template or move to a provider must be justified against these same quality gates in a new decision.
